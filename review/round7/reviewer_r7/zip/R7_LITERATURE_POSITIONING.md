# Final literature positioning notes (2026-10-08)

These are external-context notes for manuscript positioning, not evidence for the project’s measured claims.

1. Roy & Vetterli (EUSIPCO 2007), “The Effective Rank: A Measure of Effective Dimensionality.”
   The standard effective-rank construction exponentiates entropy of normalized singular values. Mode-I/II should distinguish that definition from the project’s g statistic based on the amplitude participation ratio divided by dimension.

2. Handke et al. (ICML 2026), “The Entropic Signature of Class Speciation in Diffusion Models.”
   They identify semantic commitment regimes with class-conditional entropy in diffusion models. This is useful precisely because it is a different observable from the project’s researcher-defined semantic decoding/RSA and from passive seed predictability. Cite it as motivation for G3, not as validation that the current telemetry has measured a committor.

3. Chi et al. (ACL 2026), “MTA: Multi-Granular Trajectory Alignment for Large Language Model Distillation.”
   MTA explicitly aligns representation transformation trajectories across depth rather than only fixed layers. This provides contemporary context for treating trajectory/tangent preservation under distillation as a distinct object from static representation similarity.

4. Sun et al. (ICML 2026), “Anatomy of Massive Activations and Attention Sinks.”
   A small number of extreme channels can have persistent global effects in Transformer representations. This supports keeping the massive-activation robustness check as a caveat for uncentred spectral statistics.

5. Turri et al. (arXiv 2026), “Few Channels Draw The Whole Picture: Revealing Massive Activations in Diffusion Transformers.”
   Reports functionally important sparse massive channels in diffusion transformers. This makes the optional Mode-III channel-removal/mean-centering control especially relevant before assigning universal meaning to s1-dominated statistics.

Use these works to position, delimit, or motivate future tests. Do not use them to upgrade any blocked CoS-PAFS claim.
